Which one to choose

  • You want unlimited data: Saily was cheaper in most destinations, states its full-speed allowance and has no hotspot restrictions.
  • You want a fixed amount of data: Airalo was cheaper for 10 GB in most destinations and sells 7- and 15-day plans and 50 GB.
  • Your dates might change: Saily's fixed plans almost all last 30 days, so an extra few days won't cost you.
  • You're going to Mexico or Canada with fixed data in mind: Airalo, by a wide margin.
  • Several European countries: it depends on your route — see which countries each Europe plan covers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Saily cheaper than Airalo?

It depends on the plan. Across 12 destinations checked between 29 September and 10 October 2026, Saily was cheaper for a 7-day unlimited plan in 8 of 12, while Airalo was cheaper for 10 GB valid 30 days in 8 of 12. Most differences were under $1.

Which is better for unlimited data, Saily or Airalo?

Saily had the cheaper 7-day unlimited plan in 8 of 12 destinations, usually by a cent, and by about $3 in Canada. It also states its full-speed allowance (5 GB a day in most countries, 3 GB in Canada and Mexico) and sells 20- and 25-day plans. Airalo was cheaper for a week in Japan, Turkey, France and Mexico.

Which is better for fixed data?

Airalo was cheaper for 10 GB valid 30 days in 8 of 12 destinations, by up to about $8 in Canada, and sells shorter 7- and 15-day plans and 50 GB plans in most countries. Saily was a cent cheaper in Japan, Thailand, the UK and France.

Which has the better Europe plan?

Airalo's Europe plan covers more countries (41, including the western Balkans and Ukraine), and its EU + UK plan is cheaper if you stay in the EU and the UK. Saily's Europe plan covers 35 countries including the UK, Switzerland and Turkey, and had the cheaper 7-day unlimited plan. Neither Europe plan covers Kosovo; Saily's separate Balkans plan does.

Who makes Saily?

Saily is made by Nord Security, the company behind NordVPN. Its app adds security features such as a virtual location, an ad blocker and protection against malicious sites.

Can I have both on my phone?

Yes. Most eSIM phones can store several eSIMs, so you can install one from each and switch the data line in your settings.
